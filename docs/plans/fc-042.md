## Context

FC-032 shipped a working backtest engine (PRs #35, #45): it replays the live strategy code over historical Alpaca data, validated against 204 real fills (81% strike reproduction, 100% delta-band accuracy) and against live behavior (the gap filter's blocking of NVDA in Jun–Jul 2026 matches production, which sold zero NVDA puts in the same window).

Three things now stand between "working engine" and "engine that answers our money questions":

1. **Runs cost ~25 min/symbol cold.** `ChainStore` (the parquet cache) exists but is constructed nowhere, and contract discovery fetches every strike from $50 to $390 when only ±15% of spot can ever match the delta band (~70% waste, measured on NVDA 2025-10-06).
2. **Two fidelity gaps block demotion decisions on income names.** Dividends and ex-div early assignment are unmodeled. The dividend bias runs *both* ways: it flatters the wheel on the buy-and-hold comparison but **penalises it on the absolute gates that produce `unfit`** — on a 5–7% yielder the missing dividends alone can consume the entire 4% risk-free floor.
3. **The highest-value open question is now empirically answerable.** The June–July 2026 walkthrough (see `docs/investigations/`) showed NVDA blocked ~90% of the month by the gap-risk filter — specifically the **40% realized-vol cap**, not gap frequency: NVDA's 30-day vol sat at 40–42% against a hard 40% line, so whether it traded on a given day was threshold noise. NVDA is 155 of 241 lifetime put legs; AMD has been blocked on 47% of scans (FC-002). The filter double-counts risk that delta-based strike selection already prices, with a binary cliff and no graduated response. Whether the current calibration is right is a question the engine can now answer with data instead of opinion.

## Goals

- Track A: warm backtest runs in **minutes, not hours** (cache + strike-window).
- Track B: **published A/B studies** for the gap filter (FC-002) and the premium floor (FC-034) — evidence, not code changes.
- Track C: dividends and early assignment modeled; income names judgeable.
- Track E: the two known production bugs (FC-036, FC-035) fixed behind their own small PRs, with the FC-036 fix **quantified by the engine before it is enabled**.

**A2. Strike-window filter in `get_contract_universe`.** Fetch only strikes within ±25% of the as-of close, **extended upward to `max(spot, cost_basis) × 1.25` when shares are held** — covered calls must clear cost basis, and a naive spot-window would starve the call side of exactly the underwater positions that need rolling. Alpaca's contracts endpoint accepts `strike_price_gte/lte`.
*Acceptance (as drafted):* NVDA single-day universe drops from ~148 contracts to <60 with **identical** chain output in the delta band (assert same selected candidate on 5 sampled days); parity spot-check unchanged.

*Acceptance (revised during execution — see Execution note A2):* the `<60`
target is **not achievable and was derived from one unrepresentative day**. It
is replaced by a percentage-reduction target plus the identity requirement,
which is the criterion that actually protects correctness:

- Delta-band chain output **identical** on 5 sampled days, and a full-month
  replay windowed vs unbounded produces an identical ledger and equity curve.
- Universe reduction reported honestly **through the shipped code path** (i.e.
  including the cost-basis/low anchors the simulator passes), not spot-only.
- Parity spot-check unchanged, measured rather than assumed.

### Track A — `fc-042-track-a` (A1 + A2, one PR)

- **PR:** https://github.com/memon1987/options_wheel/pull/49
- **Commits:** `322c75b` (implementation), `37fd2dc` (adversarial-review fixes)
- **Date:** 2026-07-18
- **Tests:** 504 → 542, full suite green.

**A1 — measured.** 1-month NVDA backtest (2025-10-01..10-31, 23 decision days),
counted by instrumenting the provider rather than timing:

| | option-bar calls | contract-discovery calls | wall clock |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cold | 23 | 23 | 21.0s |
| Warm | **0** | **0** | **0.2s** |

The warm run reproduced the cold run's equity curve and ledger exactly.

**A2 — measured, and where the plan was wrong.** Three corrections:

1. **`<60 contracts` is unachievable and was sampled from one day.** ±25% on
   the plan's own reference day (NVDA 2025-10-06) gives **64**, not <60. The
   universe is far larger on other dates — 932 contracts on 2026-01-12, 608 on
   2026-03-09 — so no fixed percentage window hits an absolute count of 60 in
   general (±15% on 2026-03-09 still leaves 176). The plan's two acceptance
   numbers (±25% *and* <60) are mutually inconsistent. ±25% was kept: the
   failure mode of a too-tight window is a silently different backtest, not a
   slow one.

2. **The reduction must be quoted through the shipped path.** The window the
   simulator actually requests is widened by the cost-basis ceiling and the low
   anchor. Spot-only figures overstate the saving:

   | Window | Spot-only cut | **Shipped cut** |
   |---|---|---|
   | 1 month | 57.5% | **49.1%** |
   | 6 months | 49.2% | **18.5%** |

   The saving **decays as the window lengthens**, because the anchors widen
   with it. For Track B's 2024-02→present studies the strike filter will
   contribute little; A1's cache is what makes those runs affordable.

3. **The `~25 min/symbol` figure does not describe a 1-month window** — that is
   ~21s cold. It is consistent with a full 2024-02→present history (~600
   decision days), doubled by the sensitivity pass.

**Identity, proved three ways** (all against a genuinely unbounded baseline):
delta-band quotes byte-identical on 5 sampled days; a full-month replay
windowed vs unbounded produced an identical ledger and equity curve; and
`parity_check.py --limit 40` returned identical verdicts both ways
(19 exact / 15 close / 6 diverged).

**Notes / deviations.**

- **`Simulator` does not construct a `ChainStore`.** It receives an
  already-built `ChainBuilder` and does not own it. The store is owned by
  `ChainBuilder` and wired at the application entry point (`evaluate`).
  Caching is opt-in rather than default-on so a unit test can never read a
  chain another test wrote.
- **The cache key needed more than `(symbol, date)`.** Chain content also
  depends on the DTE reach, the strike window, and — critically — the pricing
  model, since bid/ask/delta/iv are *computed*, not fetched. Each file records
  the request it answers plus a model fingerprint, and a read is a hit only
  when the file provably covers the request. **This matters for Track C:** C1
  (dividend yields) and C3 (spread recalibration) both change cached values,
  and without the fingerprint every study Track B ran would silently mix two
  pricing models. Bump the fingerprint's schema version if the *derivation*
  changes rather than its inputs.
- **Open follow-up — build chains lazily.** Every remaining wart descends from
  `_build_chains` materialising the whole window before the day loop: with no
  position in existence, cost basis has to be *bounded* rather than read, which
  is what widens the window and decays the A2 saving on long runs. Building
  chains per-day inside the loop, against the broker's real cost basis and
  real open-position strikes, would make the window tight again and remove the
  lookahead question entirely. Not attempted here — it changes the adapter
  contract and belongs in its own FC.
- Cache entries are keyed one file per `(symbol, date)`, so alternating between
  two pricing models re-fetches each time rather than keeping both. Not a
  concern for Track B (which sweeps *strategy* config, not chain model params).
- `tools/backtesting/coverage_report.py` and `tools/diagnostics/parity_check.py`
  were left unwired for the cache (out of scope); they inherit the strike
  window, and parity was measured unchanged as above.
