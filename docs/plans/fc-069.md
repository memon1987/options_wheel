### Item 1 — `max_total_positions: 10` · pre-tag: **REVIVE** (enforce in `select_batch`)

**(a) What it is.** A global cap on concurrent option positions. `config/settings.yaml:54` (`10`), property `src/utils/config.py:316–318`, validation `:161–163`.

**(b) Verified status: no global cap exists on the live path.** Consumers today: `wheel_engine._can_open_new_positions` (`:257`) and `get_strategy_status` (`:597`) — both deleted by FC-068; `risk_manager.py:52/:307/:354` — all inside methods with zero production callers (see item 7); `portfolio_tracker.py:401/:440` — CLI-only (`main.py status/report`, not deployed); `/config` exposes it as `max_positions` (`deploy/cloud_run_server.py:1069`); stale copy in `deploy/kubernetes/deployment.yaml:120`. Read of `select_batch` (`execution_engine.py:460–599`) confirms its only limits are per-underlying dedup, the calls share ledger, and put buying power. **Nothing counts positions.** Inventory claim confirmed.

**(c) Recommendation: REVIVE.**
- *Strongest FOR:* buying power is the only breadth limit on puts today. It is a *collateral* constraint, not a *count* constraint — a volatile morning that halves collateral requirements, a margin-setting change at Alpaca, or a future universe/config change could legally deploy the whole account across many puts in one batch. A count cap is the blunt, cheap, last-line guard against catastrophic breadth, and it is the single cheapest control on this inventory to put on the real path.
- *Strongest AGAINST:* in ten months of live trading, BP exhaustion and the 14-symbol universe (6 effectively tradeable, FC-048) have kept concurrent positions well under 10 — the cap has never been the binding constraint, and `10` was inherited, never chosen. Reviving it adds a behavior change on the money path (its own review cycle) to enforce a bound the account's own cash already approximates. If the honest answer is "we'd never hit it," reviving is ceremony — and DELETE is the consistent choice with the rest of this sweep.

**(d) Cost.**
- *REVIVE (S):* in `select_batch`, count open `us_option` positions from the positions snapshot (already fetched when calls are present; put-only batches gain one `get_positions` call — or reuse `/run`'s existing snapshot) plus batch-selected so far; when `count ≥ max_total_positions`, drop with a new `DROP_MAX_TOTAL_POSITIONS` reason (extends the closed `DROP_REASONS` enum, flows into FC-068's `selection_dropped` tally and P4 decision records for free). Tests: cap fires at boundary; cap is mutation-checked (raise the count, assert the drop). **Sub-decision, flagged:** does the cap count/block covered calls? The dead check counted *all* option positions and blocked *all* new ones. Recommended: count all short option positions toward the cap but **never block a covered call on already-held shares** (a call on stock we own reduces risk; blocking it strands the shares uncovered — the exact state FC-065 makes legible to avoid). Operator may prefer the original strict semantics; say which.
- *DELETE (S):* knob + property + validation + `/config` field + the dead readers (which die in item 7 / already-dead `portfolio_tracker` path anyway).

**(e) Blast radius if deleted:** `/config`'s `max_positions` field (verified: no dashboard consumer reads it — grep of `dashboard/` finds none); `tests/test_config.py:34`; k8s yaml (stale artifact either way — see Open questions Q8).

**(f) Decision:** ☐ confirm REVIVE ☐ flip to DELETE — operator. If REVIVE: sub-decision on call counting (recommended: calls count, never blocked): ☐ accept ☐ strict-original.
